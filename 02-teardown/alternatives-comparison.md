# Competitor comparison: Revolut, Trade Republic, DEGIRO and bunq

Three alternatives a student in the Netherlands is likely to consider. Revolut figures come from my own app (Primary). Competitor figures come from each provider's official fee or help pages (Secondary, checked Oct 19, 2026). I first gathered figures from third-party reviews on Oct 18, then replaced them with official sources because several reviews were wrong or out of date.

## Cost of a small first purchase

| | Revolut (in-app) | Trade Republic | DEGIRO (NL) | bunq |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commission on a manual buy | 1 commission-free stock or ETF trade a month on Standard (banner, S26 and S43); later trades not seen in-app | No commission | Core Selection ETFs: €0; other ETFs: €2 | 0.99% per trade after a 3-month promotion; 0.79% on Pro, 0.49% on Elite |
| Other per-trade cost | Currency exchange on US stocks: €0.09 on a $10 buy (S34) | €1 external settlement cost per trade, including fractional trades | €1 handling fee per trade | None stated |
| Recurring investing | Investment plans shown as "No commission" (S43, S44) | Savings plans: no fee on purchases; €1 on sales | Not checked | Not checked |
| Yearly account cost | None seen | None stated | Connectivity fee 0.25% of portfolio value, capped at €2.50 per exchange per year; Euronext Amsterdam and Brussels free | Not checked |
| Source | Own app, Oct 18 to 19 | Official support centre (via search summaries) | Official NL price list, dated 01.10.2025 | Official help centre (via search summaries) |

## What this tells me

- **Revolut is not the outlier on fees.** A €10 first purchase costs about €1 at Trade Republic and DEGIRO, about €0.10 at bunq, and nothing on Revolut's monthly free trade. This weakened H1 before the interviews, and the interviews confirmed it: no student was surprised by a fee.
- **Every provider offers a cheap long-term route** (savings plans, Core Selection, investment plans). The difference I could not see from fee pages is how each app helps a beginner choose *between* similar funds. That question went into the interviews.
- **Not compared:** where these products sit in each app and how many taps they take. Only one interviewee used a competitor (P1, Trading 212), so this stays an open question in the [PRD](../05-prd/prd.md#12-open-questions).

## Limits

- Trade Republic and bunq figures come from official help pages read through search summaries, not the full price lists.
- Fees differ by country, plan and date; these are Netherlands figures as of Oct 19, 2026.
- All links are in [sources.md](../sources.md).
