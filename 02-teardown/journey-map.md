# Journey map: a student's first fund purchase

Nielsen Norman Group format: actor, scenario and expectations, phases, actions, feelings and opportunities. I drafted it from my walkthrough on Oct 18 with guessed feelings, then corrected the feelings with the six interviews on Oct 19.

![Journey map: confidence drops at "choose a fund"](../assets/journey-map.png)

- **Actor:** a student in the Netherlands or EEA who is a first-time or early-stage investor.
- **Scenario:** from opening the Invest tab to a first purchase they feel confident about.
- **Expectation:** quick, clear about cost, and suited to a long-term goal.

## Phases

Confidence on a scale of 1 (unsure) to 5 (confident). "My guess" is what I expected after the walkthrough; "Interviews" is what students described.

| Phase | Screens | What the student does | My guess | Interviews | What students said |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Arrive | S01 to S05, S35, S39 | Opens the Invest tab inside the bank app; scrolls past single stocks, top movers and a feed | 4 | **4** | The Invest tab inside the bank app made starting feel easy (P2, P3, P5) |
| 2. Sign up | S06 to S14 | Goal, income and net worth questions, passport number, acknowledgements, a review wait | 2.5 | **3** | Heavy or repeated for three students; nobody quit (P1, P2, P3) |
| 3. Choose a fund | S15 to S30, S37, S38, S40 to S45 | Searches "S&P 500", sees three near-identical funds, opens one, compares in their head | 2 | **1.5 to 2** | Could not tell funds apart; both who had never invested left the app here (P4, P6) |
| 4. Place the order | S31 to S34 | Enters an amount, checks it, sees costs before Submit | 3 | **4** | Simple once a choice is made; nobody was surprised by fees |
| 5. Afterwards | not observed | Holds; checks the price | none | **3** | Checked the price more often than expected (P3 only) |

**Headline:** I expected the lowest points to be signing up and choosing. The interviews put the single lowest point at choosing between near-identical funds; signing up was annoying but not a reason to stop.

## Opportunities

| Rank | Opportunity | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Help students tell funds on the same index apart, in plain words, at the moment of choice | 5 of 6 students; screens S40 and S44 |
| 2 | Say plainly what could be lost and how to take money out before a first order | P1, P4, P6 |
| 3 | Keep engagement features (top movers, Buy suggestions) from pushing first-timers to trade | S03, S27, S39; FCA research (UK context). Treated as a guardrail, not a problem to fix |

**Caveat:** the actions come from one walkthrough by me; the feelings come from six interviews. Early evidence, not proof. The same map, with one student note per phase, is frame 1 of the [Miro board](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVEYcX4L0=/?moveToWidget=3458764687765872141).
