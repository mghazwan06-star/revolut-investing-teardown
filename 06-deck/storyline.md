# Deck storyline (ghost deck)

Built Oct 19, 2026 using methods from the consulting slide skills pack Muhammad added (SCQA opening, Pyramid storyline, action titles, ghost deck, executive summary). Every number traces to [synthesis](../03-research/synthesis.md), [PRD](../05-prd/prd.md) or the teardown log.

## SCQA

- **Situation:** Revolut puts investing inside the bank app, and cheap index funds are available to EEA customers (Secondary, 2025 annual results).
- **Complication:** students who decide to invest long-term search "S&P 500", see three near-identical funds, cannot tell them apart, and leave.
- **Question:** how can Revolut help a first-time student choose a fund with confidence, without giving advice?
- **Answer:** show the facts the app already holds side by side, in plain words, with no ranking, and a two-line risk summary before the first order.
