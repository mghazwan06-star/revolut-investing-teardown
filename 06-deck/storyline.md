# Deck storyline

Before designing any slide I agreed the storyline as a ghost deck: the argument as a chain of action titles, using consulting storyline methods (SCQA opening, Pyramid Principle, action titles). Every number traces to the [synthesis](../03-research/synthesis.md), the [PRD](../05-prd/prd.md) or the [teardown log](../02-teardown/teardown-log.md).

## SCQA

- **Situation:** Revolut puts investing inside the bank app, and cheap index funds are available to EEA customers (Secondary, 2025 annual results).
- **Complication:** students who decide to invest long-term search "S&P 500", see three near-identical funds, cannot tell them apart, and leave.
- **Question:** how can Revolut help a first-time student choose a fund with confidence, without giving advice?
- **Answer:** show the facts the app already holds side by side, in plain words, with no ranking, and a two-line risk summary before the first order.

## Title chain (8 slides)

| # | Action title | Exhibit | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Students stall at choosing a fund, not at fees. A neutral side-by-side view could fix it. | Executive summary: SCQA plus three reasons and next step | Solid |
| 2 | I tested four guesses with an app walkthrough and six student interviews, using a rule set in advance. | Process row plus sample by segment | Solid |
| 3 | Students lose the most confidence at one step: choosing between near-identical funds. | Journey map | Solid, n = 6 |
| 4 | Five of six students struggled to pick between similar funds. None were surprised by fees. | Evidence grid | Solid, n = 6 |
| 5 | Search "S&P 500" and three funds appear, with nothing that says how they differ. | Annotated screenshot | Solid |
| 6 | Only one of my starting guesses held up, and in a narrower form than I wrote it. | Hypothesis scorecard | Solid |
| 7 | The fix uses facts the app already has: side by side, in plain words, with no ranking. | Four design principles plus flow | Proposal |
| 8 | Success means more confident first purchases without more trading, tested first with a prototype. | Metric, guardrails, rollout, limits | Proposal; baselines to be measured |

Read as a paragraph, the titles argue: the stall is at fund choice (1), here is how I know (2 to 5), my own guesses were mostly wrong (6), so the fix targets choice with existing data (7), and here is how I would know it worked (8).
